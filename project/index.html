Click the game canvas, then hold left or right arrow, keypad 4 or 6, or A and D to rotate. Hold up or down arrow, keypad 8 or 2, or W and S to thrust. Propellant is finite, and the lander gets lighter as fuel burns. Land on the green pad with low speed and nearly upright.

Mission

Use the steerable rocket engine to slow your descent and reach the green pad. Propellant is limited, and the lander gets lighter as it burns fuel. Watch for an asteroid on some flights. Touch down upright with a vertical speed under 1.8 m/s and horizontal speed under 1.1 m/s.

Lunar gravity is 1.62 m/s². Vacuum has no usable parachute braking, so this version uses an honest retro-thrust brake instead.

Final approach starts with 300 kg of descent propellant left from an 8,200 kg tank. Fuel percentage is relative to that original load.

Physics notes. The lander uses fixed-step translational dynamics, lunar gravity, finite propellant, variable mass, and steerable rocket thrust. The energy readout remains visible, but is marked open because thrust transfers energy into the lander's motion; it is not a closed-system conservation test. Momentum is not conserved during powered flight. Touchdown is evaluated before the terrain collision response. The legacy scenes and their original integrator/conservation demonstrations remain available under “Other scenes and simulation settings.”
